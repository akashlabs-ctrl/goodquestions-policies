GoodQuestions Publisher: Data-Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Submit a request

Email goodquestionsai@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request - GoodQuestions Publisher. Include your Instagram username and enough context to identify the records, such as a post/comment link or the approximate interaction date.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, verification codes, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information. If you are the account operator, identify the connected account and whether you are requesting an integration disconnect, data removal, or both.

2. Verification and scope

The operator will review the request and may ask for limited proof that you control the relevant account. Verification must not require your Instagram password or access token. The operator will identify relevant records in the active application, audit history and retained backups.

3. Manual removal or anonymization

This version has no automated deletion endpoint or one-click purge. Database/audit history is append-only during ordinary operation, so removing or anonymizing personal data requires a controlled administrator process. The operator must address applicable personal data in stored comments, drafts, provider request/response audits and other related records, while identifying any limited records that need retention under applicable legal or security requirements.

The operator will communicate the result or any relevant retention limits. A request is not considered completed merely because a comment was hidden from a review screen or the integration was paused.

4. Backups and restoration

Private backups are retained without an automatic purge schedule and require separate review. The operator must account for applicable backup copies and ensure a later restore does not reintroduce data already removed or anonymized. Completion must not be claimed until the relevant active records and retained copies have been addressed or any justified retention limits have been explained.

5. Data controlled by other platforms

This request concerns data held by GoodQuestions Publisher. It does not automatically delete the original public Instagram comment/post, your Instagram account, Telegram records, or independently retained provider data. Use each platform's own controls or request process for data it controls. The operator can explain which service providers were involved and what additional requests may be applicable.

6. Current DM behavior

Although the messages webhook field is subscribed, this release ignores DM notifications and has no DM content archive or DM reply workflow. Operational/security metadata and any email request you send can still require review.
