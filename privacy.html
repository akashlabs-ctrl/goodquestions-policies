GoodQuestions Publisher: Privacy Notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Scope and contact

GoodQuestions Publisher is the private tool used by the operator of @arreyaar.ai to manage its own Instagram professional account. It is not a public service for connecting other businesses' accounts. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to goodquestionsai@gmail.com.

2. Information the tool processes

  • Instagram account, post, comment and author identifiers; usernames; public comment text; and relevant post captions or saved slide text.
  • When DM handling is enabled, incoming private message text, message and participant identifiers, delivery/echo metadata, and bounded recent conversation context used for private reply review.
  • Owner-provided topics, account instructions and feedback; generated drafts; proposed replies; and approval, rejection and publishing decisions.
  • Telegram owner/chat identifiers used for private review and notifications.
  • Operational records, including timestamps, delivery/deduplication state, AI request/response audit records, usage estimates and error/status metadata.
  • Private authorization credentials and configuration needed to connect the owner's Instagram, Telegram and service-provider accounts.

DM handling requires separate operator activation. When enabled, new notifications are stored even while reply processing is paused. Eligible nonsensitive text and up to six recent messages can be sent to OpenAI after operator consent; private replies require the owner's Telegram approval and a fresh check of the standard 24-hour messaging window. Attachments are not interpreted and unsolicited outreach is not supported. Please do not put passwords, access tokens, financial details or other sensitive information in comments or DMs.

3. Why information is used

The tool supports creating and reviewing the owner's posts, receiving relevant comments and, when enabled, private messages, preparing contextual replies, preventing duplicate actions, enforcing approval and spending controls, maintaining an audit trail, and investigating operational problems. The app has no data-selling or advertising-audience functionality.

4. Platforms and service providers

  • Meta/Instagram: supported APIs receive publishing or reply requests when authorized. Public content remains subject to Instagram's own settings and policies.
  • Telegram: the owner receives private draft/review messages, which can include the original comment, post context, eligible incoming DM text and proposed public or private response. Detected sensitive DM text is withheld from previews for manual Instagram handling.
  • OpenAI: when real AI generation is enabled, owner inputs and relevant context are sent to the configured API. For comment drafting, this can include comment text and post context. With separate DM consent, it can also include nonsensitive private text and bounded recent conversation context. Detected contact information or sensitive terms are escalated before a comment or DM AI call, but this is not a guarantee of detecting every sensitive item.
  • AWS: the private application/database/media are hosted on the existing Mumbai server. Temporary publishing images can be stored in a private S3 bucket for Instagram retrieval.
  • GitHub Pages: the public policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub can process web request metadata under its own privacy policies. Application code, credentials, private databases and comment or DM records are not published in the policy repository.
  • Email provider: privacy/deletion requests sent to the contact address are processed through that email service.

Provider terms, security practices and retention policies also apply. Processing can occur outside your country. This notice does not promise that third-party providers retain no data.

5. Storage and retention

Business and audit information is stored in private SQLite databases and retained private backups. Generated media can also be kept for review and audit. There is currently no automatic purge schedule for the database/audit history or retained backups; removal or anonymization requires operator action. Older backups require separate review and cleanup.

The owner can independently pause comment or DM processing without discarding enabled incoming records. DM text/context and provider request/response audits are included in the private storage/backup retention described above. Callback diagnostics are designed not to record raw webhook bodies, authorization secrets or verification URLs, but the private business/audit records described above can contain personal data.

6. Controls and security

The tool uses private configuration files, restricted storage, HTTPS, signed webhook validation, account-scoped processing, owner-only Telegram controls and approval gates. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. The operator can pause comment and DM processing. In the approved comment workflow, AI prepares eligible replies and the owner reviews them in Telegram; a public Instagram reply is sent only after the owner's explicit approval. The DM workflow similarly requires explicit owner approval for every private reply. Selective automatic sending is not enabled and would require separate activation.

7. Requests and deletion

You can contact the operator about information associated with you or request its removal. Requests are handled manually and may require limited proof of account control. See the data-deletion instructions for the process, backup considerations and records that may need to be retained under applicable legal or security requirements.

Deleting data held by this tool is separate from removing public content on Instagram or records held independently by other providers. Use those platforms' own controls for content and account data they control.

8. Policy pages and changes

These static policy pages do not embed analytics, tracking scripts or third-party content, and do not set cookies. The operator will update this notice when the tool's data handling changes.
